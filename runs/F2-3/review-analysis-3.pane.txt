● My verdict on round 3 is REVISE; the review is written to orders/review-analysis-3.md.

  The main reason is one constraint the analysis still misses. Each card's title and content come from memories and memory_maps, which teammates in other tenants can't read. Opening up only work_items and its history
  would give a team board and a "waiting on me" list of cards with no titles.

  The other findings:
  - Second workflow version can't be saved: the unique constraint on (team_id, schema_version), with schema_version defaulting to 1, blocks it for every team.
  - Rebase can still strand items: statuses with no mapping go into a read-only column with no allowed moves out. The doc also doesn't say what happens to pending sign-offs on a rebased item.
  - Workflow-change history doesn't fit: it's placed on the version row, but history rows belong to a single item, so it has to go somewhere else.
  - Insert rule is too wide: opening work-item inserts to any team member would let a teammate create rows stamped with another tenant's ID. Inserts should stay tenant-only.
  - Nits: two typos (approacher_roles, rl_fix) and an undefined phrase in R8.
