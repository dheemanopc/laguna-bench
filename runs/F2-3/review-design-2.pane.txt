● I sent the design back for revision (REVISE). The review is in orders/review-design-2.md.

  The five round-1 findings are all addressed, but three new problems block building on it:

  1. Rebase can leave an item stuck. The design allows rebasing an item whose current status has no mapping. It then sits on the new version in a status that version doesn't define, so no move out exists. That breaks R4
     ("items never get stuck").
  2. Sign-off requests can go stale or double-fire. When quorum is reached the item moves to to_status, but nothing checks that it is still in from_status. A move or rebase in between could leave the item in the wrong
     place. Nothing stops two pending requests on one item, or two simultaneous approvals both completing the move.
  3. Database permissions contradict the flows. The new tables only get read and insert permission, yet the design updates sign-off requests and the team's live-workflow pointer. The workflow change log is read-only, yet
     the design writes to it.

  The other two findings don't block:
  - One output field is described backwards.
  - Whether an item counts as finished ("terminal") should be judged by the workflow the item is pinned to, not the team's current one.
  - Naming is inconsistent: approver_tenant_id vs *_identity_id elsewhere.
